Installation process

What happens on the day

Arrival

Our engineer arrives at the agreed time, introduces themselves and walks the property with you to confirm sensor positions.

Installation

The hub, sensors, door contact and sirens are fitted neatly. Being wireless, there's minimal drilling and no mess.

Commissioning

Every device is tested, zones are named and the system is connected to the app and monitoring.

Demonstration

We show you how to arm, disarm and use the app, and set up codes and keyfobs for everyone in the household.

Completion

We leave you with a fully working system and our direct number for support.

Before your system is installed

  • Make sure someone over 18 is home for the installation
  • Have a smartphone ready to set up the app
  • Clear access to the areas where sensors and sirens will go
  • A plug socket near the hub location (we'll advise during the survey)
  • Let us know about pets so we can fit pet-tolerant sensors

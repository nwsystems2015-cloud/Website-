About NW Security Systems

Independent security installers based in Widnes, Cheshire.

Local engineers, serious systems

NW Security Systems Limited designs, installs and maintains CCTV, access control, intruder alarms and door entry systems for homes and businesses across the North West.

Our work ranges from domestic driveway cameras to hospital access control, port CCTV, ANPR weighbridges and full-height turnstiles. Whatever the size of the job, you deal directly with the engineers who design and fit it.

We take on new installations, upgrades and takeovers of existing systems, and we support what we fit long after the job is done.

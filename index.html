FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Widnes and cover Cheshire, Merseyside, Greater Manchester, Lancashire and the wider North West, and we take on larger commercial projects across the UK.

How much does a CCTV system cost?

Every system is designed around the property, so prices depend on the number of cameras, cable runs and recording. We offer a free site survey and a clear, itemised quote with no hidden extras.

Can I rent an alarm instead of buying one?

Yes. Our alarm subscription gets you a professionally installed wireless alarm with app control for a fixed monthly fee and no upfront cost. See the Rent an Alarm page for details.

Can I view my cameras on my phone?

Yes. All our CCTV systems can be set up for remote viewing on your phone, tablet or PC, and we set the app up for you on the day.

Do you offer 24/7 monitoring?

Yes. We offer professionally monitored CCTV and alarm options, so alerts are seen and acted on around the clock.

Can you take over or upgrade an existing system?

Yes. We regularly take over, repair and upgrade systems fitted by other installers, often reusing existing cabling to keep costs down.

How long does an installation take?

Most homes are completed in a day. Commercial projects are planned in stages to keep disruption to a minimum.
